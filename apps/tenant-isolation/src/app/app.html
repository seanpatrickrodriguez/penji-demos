Lantern Bay Network is a hub over two organizations. Westfield Family Center oversees a smaller organization of its own, Westfield North Outreach. Oversight reaches down the tree: an assignment at a tenant covers it and everything under it, and nothing above it or beside it.

The rules, generated

The platform's part is written once and serves every product. The rest is generated from the configuration: which roles hold each kind of record's edit permission under the access policy, and what values each record's form accepts. A test fails if the deployed file falls out of step with the configuration.
